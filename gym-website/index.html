FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

What are PowerFit Gym operating hours?

+

We are open 7 days a week! Monday to Saturday from 5:30 AM to 10:30 PM, and Sundays from 7:00 AM to 8:00 PM.

Do I get a free trial pass before buying a membership?

+

Yes! We offer a 3-Day Free Trial Pass for all new residents in Bhubaneswar, which includes full gym floor access and 1 complimentary trainer consultation.

Are group fitness classes (Yoga, Zumba) included in the basic plan?

+

Group classes are included in our PRO Performance and VIP Elite passes. Basic pass holders can upgrade or purchase single-class drop-in passes anytime.

Can I pause or freeze my membership if I travel?

+

Yes, all 6-month and annual membership holders can freeze their pass for up to 30 days per year free of charge.
